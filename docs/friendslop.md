# Friendslop — the definition (ported from Den of Thieves)

This is the working definition of the genre carried over from the previous Friendslop project
(Den of Thieves). It was distilled from the friendslop video essay John shared plus his standing
design feedback. The raw essay transcript is archived in
`../../Den of Thieves/docs/design-beliefs.md`.

## What friendslop is

Proximity-voice-based multiplayer cooperative experiences with a heavy emphasis on
**self-sabotaging collaboration**. Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., Content Warning, Peak. The
objective ("get thing, go there, survive the night") is a thin adhesive — a MacGuffin for the
funny moments. The supposed goal of the game takes a backseat to *playing* the game: laughing
with your friends is the actual win condition.

## The beliefs

1. **Funny is the objective.** Failure that produces a story is a SUCCESS of design.
   Stress + wackiness + friends = joy. Never patch out comedy.
2. **The physics must be real.** Genuinely simulated physics, not canned animations. Weight you
   can feel, objects that obey the world. No invisible walls, no fake gaps — if it looks
   passable it IS passable; blockers must be visible.
3. **Show, don't tell.** Minimal HUD. The world communicates. A number on screen is an
   admission of failure. Sound is first-class game design, not decoration.
4. **Self-sabotaging collaboration.** Necessary actions carry built-in risk. Asymmetric roles
   force concessions — who carries, who watches, who gets left behind. Sacrifice plays should
   be possible and hilarious.
5. **Fuck-around-and-find-out.** Don't over-explain. Vague how-tos and discoverable mechanics
   are a feature. Players chewing on everything to learn how it works IS the game.
6. **Simple, readable art.** You don't need hyperrealistic graphics to giggle. Low-poly, flat
   colors, easy to read during chaos — and it runs on integrated graphics.
7. **Accessibility is sacred.** Anyone's machine, one URL, no install, no learning-curve walls.
   The barely-plays-games friend must be able to join in minutes and laugh the loudest.
8. **Moments > rewards.** No grind, no unlock treadmill. The memories that survive years are
   the clip-worthy disasters, not quotas hit. Design for stories people retell.

## Hard platform requirements (carried over unchanged)

- Runs **in the browser** — one URL, any machine, no install.
- **Co-op multiplayer** — this project is designed for exactly 3 players (John + his 2 best friends).
- **Proximity voice chat** is the core social system (Discord is an acceptable stand-in during
  early prototypes; see the handoff doc's build order).
